# Atmospheric-escape scenarios

Status: **Phase 8 scenario release**. The output is deliberately not a verdict
about whether any planet currently has an atmosphere.

The transparent baseline is energy-limited escape:

\[
\dot M = \frac{\epsilon\pi R_p R_\mathrm{XUV}^2 F_\mathrm{XUV}}
                 {G M_p K_\mathrm{tide}}.
\]

`K_tide` uses the Erkaev et al. Roche-potential correction. The ensemble varies
heating efficiency over 0.05, 0.15 and 0.30; XUV absorption radius over 1.0,
1.1 and 1.3 optical radii; and all three XUV histories. It reports current mass
loss, lifetime-integrated lost mass, and the ratio to assumed initial envelope
fractions of 0.1%, 1% and 10% of planet mass. Ratios may exceed one because they
diagnose inconsistency in a fixed-property scenario; they are not clipped into
a claim that a real atmosphere vanished.

Measured masses, radial-velocity minimum masses, deprojected minimum-mass
scenarios and mass-radius predictions retain separate evidence labels. Upper
limits are not used as if they were measurements. Planet mass and radius are
held fixed through time.

Energy-limited escape can fail when radiative/recombination cooling,
diffusion-limited supply, chemistry, magnetic fields, stellar wind, changing
planet structure or non-hydrodynamic escape controls the flow. The integrated
values are **SCENARIOS**, not reconstructed atmospheric histories.

Forty-three of the 60 targets have an age-based XUV history and a usable mass.
Each receives 27 energy-limited combinations. Three rows with catalogue upper
limits are excluded from the escape calculation instead of treating their
limits as measurements.

Primary reference: Erkaev et al. (2007),
doi:10.1051/0004-6361:20066929.
