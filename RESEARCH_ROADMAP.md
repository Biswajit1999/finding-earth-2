# Research roadmap

## v2.0 — completed evidence architecture

- Selection-aware DR25 occurrence inference with reliability and validation.
- Composition ensemble, time-dependent climate, XUV/escape, atmosphere evidence.
- HWO precursor scenarios, mission observatory, information gain, falsification.
- Interactive observatory, manuscript, and checksum-verified data release.

## v2.x — replication and stronger calibration

- Independent reruns on clean Linux and Windows environments.
- External review of selection and reliability contracts.
- Broader host-level activity and abundance evidence.
- DOI publication and archived website snapshot.

## v3 — beyond the local candidate list

- Hierarchical stellar-population dependence and cross-survey selection models.
- Exomoon, technosignature, rogue-planet, and extragalactic detectability modules.
- A formal value-of-information scheduler linked to feasible observing proposals.

Roadmap items are research directions, not promised results or mission forecasts.
